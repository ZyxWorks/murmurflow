Write at the
speed you talk.

Hold a key, say it, let go. The words land at your cursor in any app. On your machine, for nothing.

macOS, paste into Terminal

/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZyxWorks/murmurflow/main/install.sh)"

Windows, paste into PowerShell

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZyxWorks/murmurflow/main/install.ps1 | iex

Then double-tap Control, say something, and tap once more to stop. That is the whole product.

Open source, MIT licensed. That command runs a script you can read first, and so is everything it installs.

A 200-word message is five minutes of typing. Say it instead and you are done in eighty seconds, hands still on the desk. Ten of those a day is most of an hour back, every day.

Averages, not promises: conversational speech runs about 130 to 150 words a minute, and an average typist about 40. Your own numbers are yours to check.

What you are not paying for

No subscriptionCloud dictation bills you every month for as long as you use it. This has no plan to upgrade into.
No accountNothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, nothing to cancel later.
No serverwhisper.cpp transcribes on your own machine. Your voice is never uploaded anywhere.
No dependenciesThe Python standard library, ffmpeg, and the OS. That is the whole supply chain.

M4 Pro, macOS 26, large-v3-turbo, an 11 second clip. The warm server earns its place on the first sentence after a boot, which is the one a person forms their opinion on, so the daemon pays that cost at login while nobody is waiting. Measure your own before believing any of these, including ours.

What it does not do

  • No compiled helper. Key state is polled through stdlib ctypes. No Xcode, no signing, no notarization.
  • No streaming. It would cost a permanently resident microphone to save a fraction of a batch pass.
  • No LLM on the hot path, unless you ask for one.
  • No Linux. Wayland exposes no global hotkey API at all, so the answer is honest rather than half working.

Privacy, concretely

Your voice is transcribed by your own machine and the recording is discarded the moment that is done, before anything else happens to it. The microphone closes with the key, and it cannot be left open.

The transcript is never inspected, filtered, or written down. It goes to your clipboard, then your cursor, and your previous clipboard contents are put back. Nothing here makes a network request except the one-off model download.

The source is public, and so is every claim on this page.
